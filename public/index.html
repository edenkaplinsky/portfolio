Projects

Peristaltic Vaccuum Pump
Fall 2025 - Mechanical Design class project
Inflate baloons running clockwise or simmer water at room temperature (pressure 0.026 atm) running counterclockwise. Flex TPU tube has notched inner profile to improve seal and reduce stress concentrations. 3d printed prototype, milled aluminum housing, waterjet cut steel rotors, i2c pressure sensor for vacuum jar.

Glass Map
Fall 2025 - A fairly useless object shaped like Earth’s surface at 38.06 °N, 119.90 °W
Pulled topographic data from Esri GIS, created mesh and processed in Blendr to adjust draft angles, 3d printed the map, used the plastic model to make a sand mold, lined the mold in soot, poured molten glass, annealed in kiln, sanded base flat.
