  <br>
  <span style= "color: gray;" style="font-size: 20px;"><i>Fall 2025 - Mechanical Design class project</i></span>
  <br>

<p>
  <span style="font-size: 24px;"><b>Glass Map</b></span>
  <br>
  <span style= "color: gray;" style="font-size: 20px;"><i>Fall 2025 - A fairly useless object shaped like Earth’s surface at 38.06 °N, 119.90 °W </i></span>
  <br>
  <span style= "color: gray;" style="font-size: 20px;">Pulled topographic data from Esri GIS, created mesh and processed in Blendr to adjust draft angles, 3d printed the map, used the plastic model to make a sand mold, lined the mold in soot, poured molten glass, annealed in kiln, sanded base flat.</span>
</p>
